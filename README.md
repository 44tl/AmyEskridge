# Amy Catherine Eskridge Archive

> **A comprehensive digital research archive documenting the life, scientific contributions, and legacy of Amy Catherine Eskridge (1987–2022).**  
> This project separates empirical biographical and scientific records from the speculative socio-political narratives that emerged following her death.

---

## Overview

Amy Catherine Eskridge (September 19, 1987 – June 11, 2022) was an interdisciplinary aerospace researcher, CEO of HoloChron Engineering, and Co-Founder of The Institute for Exotic Science. Born into Huntsville, Alabama's aerospace establishment—her father, Richard Eskridge, was a NASA MSFC propulsion scientist—she pursued frontier research in gravitomagnetism, electroceramics, pulsed power systems, and zero-point energy rectification.

Following her death by suicide at age 34, her biography was co-opted by the viral 2026 "Missing Scientists" conspiracy theory, prompting congressional inquiries and federal investigations. This archive presents the full empirical record, contextualizing her genuine scientific ambitions against the backdrop of her documented struggles with chronic pain and psychological decline.
